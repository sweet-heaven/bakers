Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery hours and areas? +
We deliver from 8 AM to 8 PM daily, including weekends. Our delivery radius extends up to 10km from our patisserie. For orders outside this area, a special delivery fee may apply. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2 PM.
Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options? +
Yes! We have a dedicated selection of gluten-free and vegan pastries. Our almond flour-based creations are naturally gluten-free, and we use premium plant-based ingredients for our vegan range. Please notify us of any dietary restrictions when ordering.
How far in advance should I order a custom cake? +
For custom cakes, we recommend ordering at least 48 hours in advance. For elaborate designs or large tiered cakes, we suggest 5-7 days notice. Wedding cakes require a minimum of 4 weeks advance booking to ensure perfection.
Can I modify or cancel my order? +
You can modify or cancel your order up to 24 hours before the scheduled delivery or pickup. For bespoke creations already in production, a 50% fee may apply. Please contact our concierge team as soon as possible for any changes.
Do you offer catering for events? +
Absolutely! We specialize in catering for events of all sizes - from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations. Our event team can create custom dessert tables, wedding cakes, and pastry assortments. Contact our events coordinator at events@sweetheaven.com.
What is your policy on allergens? +
We take allergens seriously. All our products are prepared in a kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, gluten, and eggs. While we take precautions to prevent cross-contamination, we cannot guarantee allergen-free products. Detailed ingredient lists are available for all items.
